Hey hey everyone, _sashkaa here 😎 

**0.15.0** is the biggest release yet. Meitou now runs natively on **Linux** and **macOS**.

We've added many, many systems to the game simulation, **optimized** a lot (and I mean, Kenshi RTS level), fixed a lot.

Many thanks to all our testers, and everyone who contributed code!

Here's a fat and juicy changelog.

**Base building**

- Production: wells, farms with growing crops, mines, research and refiners, and crafting benches with queues, quality and criticals
- Power: generators, fuel, batteries, wind generators and town power sharing
- Job lists as the original (operate, haul, build, medic, rescue and more), with the AI tab and squad AI options
- Automatic machines work unattended; operators haul outputs and fetch inputs
- Your outposts get the town panel, and public outposts draw traders
- Beds, sleeping, bed rent and town house purchases
- Turrets fire, and town guards man them when enemies are near

**Squads and the world**

- Squads tab: add, rename, delete, reorder and swap members
- Combat as the original: attack techniques, attack slots, initiative, circling and pursuit
- Stealth with the original's noticing arrows and light-level icon
- First aid, knockouts and comas follow the original's rules
- Captives: cannibals, slavers, town occupiers and fleeing refugees
- Much more of the original's dialogue, NPC-to-NPC talk and faction ranks (join the Shinobi Thieves)
- Relations change from blows, deaths, cagings and first aid; trespassing and witnessed assaults count
- Weather hurts: dust, acid rain and gas, with protection that wears
- Trade: buyback, slave trading, per-container shop stock and full item tooltips

**Also new**

- Character editor matching the original, with .bod2 import and export
- Language selection with the game's translations
- Import game: start a new world from an old save
- Graphics: skin subsurface scattering, ground and rock relief, SMAA, blood on the terrain, carried lanterns and quality presets
- Faithful mode gets the original's splash, loading screen, title windows and fonts, plus a Fixes page for vanilla bugs
- Faster big battles and threaded pathfinding

The full list is in the [release notes](https://github.com/VlcekM/MeitouClient/releases).
